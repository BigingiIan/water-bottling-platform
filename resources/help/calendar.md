## What this page is for

The Business Calendar records the days that change demand: public holidays, school terms and promotions. The forecast reads them for every product and day, so keeping the calendar up to date keeps the forecast honest.

## Tasks

### Add a movable public holiday

1. Open Business Calendar and select the Public holidays tab.
2. Select Add holiday and enter the name and date, for example Good Friday or Eid al-Fitr once announced.
3. Leave Same date every year unticked for holidays whose date moves, then select Save.

### Record this year's school terms

1. Open the School terms tab and select Add school term.
2. Enter the year, the term number and the start and end dates published by the Ministry of Education.
3. Save each of the three terms. Days outside every term count as school holidays.

### Plan a promotion

1. Open the Promotions tab and select Add promotion.
2. Choose one product, or All products, and the dates it runs.
3. Choose the type and, for a discount, the percentage, then select Save.

A promotion only tells the forecast to expect more demand. It does not change order prices.

### Correct a past entry

1. Edit or delete the entry as usual.
2. If it covers days whose sales the forecast has already learned from, you are asked for a reason.
3. Give the reason and save. The change and the reason are recorded in the audit log.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** see the month calendar and the lists of holidays, terms and promotions.
- **Full:** also add, edit and delete them, including corrections to past days with a reason.

## Common problems

- **The Forecasting page warns about school terms.** No terms are recorded for the coming weeks' year. Add them on the School terms tab.
- **A holiday is missing from the forecast.** Movable holidays aren't added automatically. Add them once the date is announced.
- **You are asked for a reason.** The change touches sales history the forecast already used. Give a short reason so the change can be traced.
