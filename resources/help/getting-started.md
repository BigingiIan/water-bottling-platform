## What AquaOps is for

AquaOps keeps a water bottling business's day in one place: what is being produced, what is in the warehouse, what customers ordered, what they owe, and what demand to expect tomorrow. Each person sees only the parts their role covers, so the screens stay focused on your own work.

## Tasks

### Sign in for the first time

1. Open the invitation email and select the link to set your password.
2. Choose a password of at least the length shown, then confirm it.
3. You land on your home page and the welcome tour starts. You can skip it and replay it from Help at any time.

### Find your way around

1. Use the sidebar on the left to open a module. On a phone, select the menu button at the top left.
2. Select the bell at the top right to see your notifications.
3. Select your initials at the top right for your profile, Help and signing out.
4. Select the ? next to any page title for a short explanation of that page.

### Change your password or notification choices

1. Open your profile menu and select My Profile.
2. Use the Password tab to change your password.
3. Use the Preferences tab to choose your landing page, list length and which alerts you receive.

### See exactly what you can do

1. Open Help.
2. Read What you can do. It lists every module and your access, worked out from your role.

## What each access level lets you do

Every module is set to one of three levels for your role.

- **No access:** the module is hidden from your sidebar and its pages are refused.
- **View:** you can open and read the module, but not change anything.
- **Full:** you can read and make changes, such as logging production or dispatching orders.

Only an administrator can change a role's access, in Roles & Permissions.

## Common problems

- **A page says you don't have access.** Your role doesn't include that module. Select Request Access on that page to ask the administrators, or send the team a request instead.
- **A button you expected is missing.** Your role probably has View rather than Full access to that module.
- **The tour keeps reappearing.** Finish or skip it once; it is then saved to your account.
- **You forgot your password.** Ask an administrator to reset it from the Staff Directory.
