## What this page is for

The dashboard is your starting point. It lists the most urgent items across your modules and shows a widget for each area your role covers.

## Tasks

### See what needs attention first

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Read Needs your attention. Critical items are listed first, then the oldest.
3. Select an item to open the page where it can be dealt with.

### Choose and reorder your widgets

1. Select Customise at the top of the dashboard.
2. Tick or untick widgets to show or hide them.
3. Use the arrows to move widgets up or down.
4. Select Save layout. Reset to role default puts the original layout back.

### Use a quick action

1. Select one of the buttons under the page title, such as Create order or Record stock count.
2. Quick actions only appear for things your role can do.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View or Full:** see the dashboard. The widgets and quick actions follow your access to each module, not to the dashboard itself.

## Common problems

- **A widget is missing.** Your role may not include that module, or the widget is hidden. Select Customise to check.
- **All widgets are hidden.** Select Customise and tick the ones you want, or reset to the role default.
- **Figures look out of date.** Widgets refresh every minute while the page is open.
