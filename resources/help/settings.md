## What this page is for

Settings hold the values the rest of AquaOps works from, such as stock thresholds, leakage limits, invoice terms and the default time to answer a request. Every change is recorded in the audit trail.

## Tasks

### Change a setting

1. Open Settings and choose a tab: General, Operations, Forecasting, Invoicing, Notifications or Security.
2. Change the value. The description under each field explains its effect.
3. Select Save settings.

### Put a tab back to its defaults

1. Open the tab.
2. Select Reset to defaults and confirm. Every setting on that tab returns to its default.

### See who changed what

1. Open Settings and select the Audit tab.
2. Filter by person, action or date to find the change.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** see settings and the audit trail.
- **Full:** also change settings.

## Common problems

- **A value won't save.** It is outside the allowed range shown under the field.
- **An alert stopped arriving.** It may be turned off on the Notifications tab, which overrides individual choices.
