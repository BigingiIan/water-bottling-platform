## What this page is for

Inventory shows stock on hand per product and warehouse line, the batches it came from, and every movement that changed it. Keeping stock-in, counts and adjustments here keeps stock levels, reorder alerts and forecasts accurate.

## Tasks

### Record stock-in

1. Open Inventory › Stock Overview.
2. Select Record Stock-In.
3. Choose the batch, product, location and units received, then save.

### Record a stock count

1. Open Inventory and select Record stock count.
2. Choose the product and location, and enter the units you counted.
3. Save. A difference from the system figure is recorded and, if large, flagged as a leakage report.

### Adjust stock

1. On the stock table, select Adjust on the line to correct.
2. Enter the change in units (negative to remove stock), choose a reason such as Count correction or Damaged, and add a note.
3. Save. The change is kept as a stock movement.

### Review a leakage report

1. Open the Leakage Reports tab.
2. Read the report's flag, loss and explanation.
3. Select Acknowledge when someone is looking into it, or Resolve with a note on what was found.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** see stock levels, batches, leakage reports and movements.
- **Full:** also record stock-in, counts and adjustments, and review leakage reports.

## Common problems

- **A product shows Low or Critical.** Its total stock is below the reorder threshold. Plan production or send the production team a request.
- **The forecast cover shows No forecast.** The forecast service has no forecast for tomorrow yet. See the Forecasting guide.
- **Stock went down unexpectedly.** Check the Movements tab; every change is listed with who made it.
