## What this page is for

Invoices are raised from dispatched orders and record what each customer owes. Issued invoices are locked so their amounts never change; mistakes are corrected by voiding and reissuing.

## Tasks

### Raise an invoice

1. Open a dispatched order.
2. Select Create invoice. A draft invoice is created from the order's lines.
3. Check the lines and VAT, then select Issue invoice. The invoice gets its number and due date.

### Record a payment

1. Open the invoice.
2. Select Record payment and enter the amount, method and reference, such as an M-Pesa code.
3. Save. Part payments leave the rest outstanding.

### Correct an issued invoice

1. Open the invoice and select Void invoice, giving a reason.
2. Raise a new invoice from the order. The voided invoice keeps its number for the record.

### Find what is overdue

1. Open Invoices and filter by Overdue.
2. Open an invoice to see the balance and the payments so far.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** see invoices, payments and what is overdue.
- **Full:** also raise, issue and void invoices and record payments.

## Common problems

- **Create invoice is missing.** The order hasn't been dispatched yet, or it already has a live invoice.
- **You can't change an issued invoice.** Issued invoices are locked. Void and reissue instead.
- **A payment won't save.** It can't be more than the balance still owed.
