## What this page is for

Forecasting shows tomorrow's expected demand for each product, from the forecast service, next to recent sales and current stock. Use it to plan production and to see how many days the stock on hand will last.

## Tasks

### Read tomorrow's forecast

1. Open Forecasting.
2. Read the forecast and stock cover for each product.
3. A cover of under a day or so means stock may run out; plan a run or send production a request.

### Generate forecasts now

1. Open Forecasting.
2. Select Generate forecasts. Forecasts are also generated automatically every evening.

### Find out why a forecast is missing

1. Read the reason shown for the product, such as missing sales history or the service being unreachable.
2. The footer's status dot shows whether the forecast service is reachable.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** see forecasts and stock cover.
- **Full:** also generate forecasts on demand.

## Common problems

- **No forecast for tomorrow.** Forecasts need 14 complete days of sales history and the day's features, and the service must be running.
- **The service is unreachable.** Ask whoever runs the forecast service to start it. Forecasts already stored stay visible.
- **A forecast looks wrong.** The model was trained on sample data; accuracy improves as real sales are recorded.
