## What requests are for

A request asks another team to do something, such as check stock, schedule a run or correct a record, without you needing access to their module. It is not a chat: every request has a sender, a team it is addressed to, a status and a thread for replies.

## Tasks

### Ask about a record you're looking at

1. Open the record, for example an order, a production run, a batch, a leakage report or a stock line.
2. Select Send request.
3. Choose the team to send it to, the priority and a due date.
4. Say what you need and by when, then select Send request.

The team sees a short summary of the record even if they can't open it themselves.

### Send a general request

1. Open Requests from the sidebar and select New request.
2. Choose the team, the type and the priority.
3. Write a subject and what you need, then send it.

### Follow up on a request you sent

1. Open Requests and select the Sent tab.
2. Open the request to see its status and replies.
3. Add a comment to answer questions or add detail.
4. Cancel it while it is still open if you no longer need it.

### Ask for access to a module

1. Open the page you need. If your role can't open it, the page says so.
2. Select Request Access. The administrators receive your request with your role and the page you tried to open.
3. You are notified when they reply. Access is changed by an administrator, never automatically.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** read the requests you sent and the requests addressed to your team.
- **Full:** also send requests, reply, and act on requests addressed to your team.

## Common problems

- **You can't find a request.** You only see requests you sent and requests addressed to your team.
- **The record link is missing.** The team can see the summary, but only people with access to that module see the link to the live record.
- **Your request was declined.** Read the reason, then reopen it once if you can add what was missing.
