## What this page is for

This area manages who can sign in and what they can do. The Staff Directory lists every account; Roles & Permissions sets each role's access to every module.

## Tasks

### Invite a new member of staff

1. Open Users & Roles and select Invite User.
2. Enter their name, email and role, then send the invitation.
3. They set their own password from the email link.

### Change someone's role or suspend them

1. In the Staff Directory, open the person's row menu.
2. Select Change role, Reset password, or Suspend.
3. Select their name to see their access and recent activity.

### Change what a role can access

1. Open Roles & Permissions.
2. In Module access, choose None, View or Full for each role and module.
3. Select Review & save, check the summary of changes, then save.

### Approve an access request

1. Open the access request from Requests or your notifications.
2. Select Review in Roles & Permissions and change the role's access there.
3. Mark the request done with a note. Nothing is granted automatically.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View:** see the staff directory and each role's access.
- **Full:** also invite staff, change roles, suspend accounts and edit permissions.

## Common problems

- **You can't lower the Administrator role's access.** The Administrator role always keeps full access.
- **You can't delete a role.** System roles can't be deleted, and custom roles can't be deleted while people still have them.
- **A change didn't take effect.** Permission changes apply from the person's next page load.
