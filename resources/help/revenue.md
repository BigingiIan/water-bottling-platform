## What this page is for

Revenue summarises money over time: what was invoiced, what was collected and what is still owed. It is read only and built from invoices and payments.

## Tasks

### Compare periods

1. Open Revenue.
2. Choose a period, or set your own From and To dates.
3. Read the totals and the chart for invoiced against collected.

### Export the figures

1. Choose the period you need.
2. Select Export CSV to download the figures for a spreadsheet.

### Check who owes money

1. Read the outstanding total on Revenue.
2. Open Invoices and filter by Overdue for the individual invoices.

## What each access level lets you do

- **View or Full:** see and export revenue. Nothing on this page can be changed.

## Common problems

- **A payment is missing from a day.** Revenue days are grouped in UTC, so late-evening payments can fall on the next day.
- **Totals differ from orders.** Revenue counts invoices, not orders. An order only counts once it is invoiced.
